Cash

All figures BBD. Prices include VAT. Contribution uses net ÷ 1.175. Input VAT recovery is not modelled Template. Salary sits in the monthly fixed line, not in the per-stop rates.

Reading these tables

Stop = one driver visit to a customer address. Touch = one warehouse handling of one bin.
Cash = money that leaves the bank because the stop happened (fuel, wear). Loaded = that cash plus the share of the FTE’s salary and vehicle fixed cost charged to the stop. Salary is paid either way, so cash answers “what does this month cost” and loaded answers “is the price right at scale”.
Gap = fee minus loaded cost on one event. Negative is deliberate: storage rent covers the FTE, not trip fees.
Borrow = bins go home for the 7-day pack window and come back — two stops, one $50. Close-out = customer keeps the contents, bins return to inventory — one stop, one $50.

05

How it runs

Customers never sign in. They book and look up by phone or booking ref. Staff use three screens: admin, warehouse, driver. Phone cameras read the barcodes.

The Valet system already runs that lifecycle. Store All already bills on Plug’n Pay. The app does not take cards, time the 7-day clocks, or capture seals and weight.

Occupancy at the facility is the $30 charge. Finance keys valet bookings to the existing Plug’n Pay account.

Month-6 rule

Scale Occupancy above break-even; churn and service green; contribution within 15% of this model; no open legal, claims or safety issue; and a forecast of cash recovery within 24 months of launch. Cost FTE #2 in the next paper.

Extend / reprice Demand is above break-even, but hours, borrow share or CAC are amber, with a written fix.

Stop Occupancy below 54% with no pipeline; cash heading through $72,000; claims or safety fail; or the offer cannot cover actual cost.

07

Risks

Demand misses break-even

Mitigation: prepaid Gate 0. Do not take the bay on amber. Stop if month-6 occupancy is under 54% with no pipeline.

Price or unit cannibalisation

Mitigation: Gate 0 is the price test. 5.5 bins ($165) sit under the $205 5×6. Script at 7–8. Report diverted unit enquiries.

Outbound hours blow the FTE

Mitigation: 7-day cap and $10/bin/day hold. Watch borrow share and hours. Reprice $50 only if hours go amber.

One person, or office will not take admin

Mitigation: FTE = driver + warehouse. Office = admin, Plug’n Pay, phone. Named backup on $250 surge. Confirm the split before Gate 0.

Damage, theft, claims

Mitigation: scans, seals, CCTV, $500/bin cap, binder before Gate 1.

Lease, insurance, or a 250-bin bay

Mitigation: 6-month break or assignment. Binder; reopen if cover > $650/mo. 500 positions on green only.

Unpaid web bookings counted as demand

Mitigation: Gate 0 counts only when existing Plug’n Pay shows prepaid and card on file against the booking ref. Do not launch until valet bookings are keyed that way.

No Coverley gate access

Mitigation: vehicle pass and a resident / property-management channel before advertising. A website dropdown is not a gate code.

08

Decision

The offer holds together. Base-case payback (~month 17) sits inside the 24-month scale test. Downside stays inside $72,000. Skip Gate 0, take a 250-tote bay, or count unpaid website bookings, and it does not.

Approve, subject to

  1. Maximum cash exposure BBD $72,000.
  2. Gate 0: 30 prepaid accounts and 165 reserved bins, counted only after Plug’n Pay shows prepaid first month and card on file against the booking ref.
  3. Gate 1: insurance binder, counsel, Coverley access, and a 6-month facility lease break, then a 500-position install on green only.
  4. One field FTE (driver + warehouse). Store All office on admin and billing. Named backup. Monthly invoice from stored bins.
  5. Month-6 go / extend / stop, including the 24-month cash-recovery test.
  6. FTE #2, SiteLink and any rollout: a separate paper.
